The program has two tracks: School & Inter Track for students in Class 6 through Intermediate 2nd Year, and College & Degree Track for UG, PG, B.Tech, MCA, B.Sc & Diploma students. No prior coding experience is required for either track.
The fee is a one-time ₹499 per student.
