Program Overview

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything students and parents need to know about the Dilshaj Infotech Skill Development Program.

The program has two tracks: School & Inter Track for students in Class 6 through Intermediate 2nd Year, and College & Degree Track for UG, PG, B.Tech, MCA, B.Sc & Diploma students. No prior coding experience is required for either track.
The registration fee is: ₹499 per student for Track 1 (School & Inter: Class 6th to 12th) and ₹999 per student for Track 2 (College & Degree: UG/PG/Diploma). This one-time fee includes 21 days of offline/online training, 14 days of dedicated project mentorship, an official verified MSME & AICTE recognized certificate, lab/learning access, and eligibility to compete for the grand prize pools (up to ₹5,00,000 combined across tracks).
The 21-day training (both Offline Classroom and Live Online batches) is scheduled for 1 hour per day during late afternoon or evening hours, so it does not clash with regular school or college timings. The exact batch timings will be communicated upon registration.
For offline classroom training at our center, lab computer systems are fully provided. For online batches, students attend remotely using their own laptop or PC with an internet connection. For the 14-day project phase, having access to a computer at home is recommended for hands-on building.
After completing classroom training, students get 14 days to build a real-world software project. Each student is assigned a mentor who provides guidance, code reviews, and milestone check-ins. Students can build individual or team projects, and the best projects compete for cash prizes.
There are separate prize pools for each track totaling up to ₹5,00,000. School & Inter Track: ₹2,00,000 total pool — 1st Prize ₹50,000, 2nd Prize ₹30,000, 3rd Prize ₹20,000 plus official winner trophies, medals & merit awards. College Track: ₹3,00,000 total pool — 1st Prize ₹1,00,000, 2nd Prize ₹50,000, 3rd Prize ₹25,000 (total ₹1,75,000 in top direct cash prizes), plus ₹1,25,000 worth gifts awarded to the top 100 students. Winners are evaluated on Innovation (25%), Technical Execution (25%), Practical Impact (20%), UI/UX (15%), and Presentation (15%).
